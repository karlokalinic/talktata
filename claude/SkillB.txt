# HR->EN Travel Year Plan

This skill turns the curriculum into an app-ready one-year program.

## Program target

- Total time: about 365 hours
- Pace: about 1 hour per day
- Learner: Croatian adult, high motivation, near-zero English
- End-of-year result: strong travel B1 with B2 foundations
- Product model: static web app, gamified, mobile-friendly, Croatian interface

## Program structure

| Phase | Weeks | Hours | Target |
|------|-------|-------|--------|
| 1 | 1-14 | about 100 | A1 survival |
| 2 | 15-32 | about 120 | A2 travel independence |
| 3 | 33-46 | about 100 | B1a independent communication |
| 4 | 47-52 | about 45 | B1b confidence and B2 foundation |

## Daily session template

Use this unless there is a special review day.

| Time | Focus |
|------|-------|
| 0-15 min | SRS review plus a small number of new items |
| 15-30 min | listening or pronunciation |
| 30-50 min | lesson or exercise block |
| 50-60 min | low-pressure input or short review |

Rules:

- keep the daily target visibly achievable
- avoid sessions longer than 60-70 minutes
- if motivation drops, switch to scenario practice instead of more theory
- weekends may be lighter, but consistency should not break

## Phase goals

### Phase 1: A1 survival

End result:

- greets people
- orders food
- checks in at a hotel
- asks for directions
- handles numbers and basic money

Priority lesson themes:

1. greetings and politeness
2. airport basics
3. hotel check-in
4. restaurant basics
5. directions
6. money and shopping basics
7. basic health and pharmacy

### Phase 2: A2 travel independence

End result:

- describes past events simply
- makes and changes plans
- handles delays and mistakes
- shops, complains politely, solves small problems

Priority lesson themes:

1. past trip stories
2. transport and tickets
3. changing plans
4. complaints and fixes
5. shopping and sizes
6. health and emergencies

### Phase 3: B1a independent communication

End result:

- speaks about life and experiences
- expresses preferences and opinions
- handles longer conversations on familiar topics
- follows the gist of familiar native speech

Priority lesson themes:

1. have you ever...?
2. present perfect vs past simple
3. opinions and recommendations
4. indirect questions
5. travel storytelling
6. phrasal verbs in context

### Phase 4: B1b confidence and B2 foundation

End result:

- uses core travel English with much less hesitation
- writes short messages and complaints
- understands more natural listening input
- is ready to continue toward B2 after year 1

Priority lesson themes:

1. full scenario roleplay review
2. weak-point repair
3. confidence speaking
4. listening push
5. review sweep of phases 1-3

## App mapping: lesson object design

The current app in `engleski/js/data.js` already provides a practical lesson schema.
When creating or expanding lessons, keep this structure:

- `id`
- `title`
- `subtitle`
- `icon`
- `locked`
- `context`
- `culturalTip`
- `phrases[]`
- `exercises[]`

Each phrase should include:

- `id`
- `en`
- `hr`
- `phonetic`
- `phoneticHr`
- `tip`
- `difficulty`

Each lesson should feel like a mini travel scene, not a grammar chapter.

## Lesson-writing rules for this app

1. A lesson should usually contain 5-8 phrases.
2. Every phrase should be sayable out loud by a beginner.
3. Exercises should mix listening, recognition, and active recall.
4. Cultural tips should reduce friction in the UK, not add trivia.
5. The first lessons must over-index on confidence and politeness.
6. New lessons should reuse earlier words where possible.
7. Locked lessons should unlock in a clear, motivational order.

## Exercise mix by level

### A1

- listen and choose
- match pairs
- simple situation choice
- short repeat-aloud prompts

### A2

- more situations
- spelling and typing
- error spotting
- short booking and complaint scenarios

### B1

- tense contrast drills
- indirect question conversion
- short opinion writing
- longer roleplay sequences

## Unlock logic

Use simple, visible gates.

- one lesson should unlock the next
- review should remain always available
- achievements should reward streaks, first completions, and scenario mastery
- phase gates should require completion, not perfection

## Progress expectations

Use these checkpoints for product messaging.

- around 100 hours: "You can survive common travel situations."
- around 220 hours: "You can handle travel more independently."
- around 320 hours: "You can explain, ask, and narrate with more confidence."
- around 365 hours: "You are functional, independent, and ready to keep improving."

## Content QA checklist

- Is this lesson useful on a real trip?
- Would the learner plausibly say these lines in the UK?
- Is the Croatian explanation simpler than the English?
- Are we teaching too much at once?
- Does the exercise set include at least one active task?
- Does this fit the current app schema without extra engineering?
